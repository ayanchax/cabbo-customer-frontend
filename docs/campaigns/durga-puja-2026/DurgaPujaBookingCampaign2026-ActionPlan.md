# Cabbo Durga Puja 2026 Campaign Action Plan

## Campaign Structure

This campaign has two separate tracks with different objectives. Do not mix the
physical standee objective with the online Puja booking objective.

1. Online Puja booking campaign: runs from 15 September to 15 October, then
   closes.
2. Offline flex/standee presence: runs at BSS and possibly SBSF as Cabbo app
   recall and adoption.

Both tracks can share the same brand identity, but they should use different
copy, QR destinations, and success metrics.

## Track 1: Online Puja Booking Campaign

### Objective

Run a time-bound Bengaluru Durga Puja booking campaign online - from 15 September to
15 October to capture Puja/pandal-hopping cab enquiries before the festival.

This track is for conversion. It should explain the Puja packages, answer
pricing questions, collect enquiries, and move interested customers into a
manual booking flow.

### Positioning

Primary message:

> Durga Puja pandal-hopping cab packages are available in Bengaluru.

Supporting message:

> Cabbo is a scheduled cab booking platform for Bengaluru-origin trips.

### Channels

- Public campaign page at `app.cabbo.co.in/durga-puja-bengaluru`
- Cabbo landing and login page campaign banner
- WhatsApp forwards and existing customer broadcasts
- Instagram and Facebook posts
- BSS social media posts or shout-outs about this campaign.
- SBSF social media mention if confirmed

### QR And Link Target

Online campaign links should open the public Durga Puja campaign page or a
WhatsApp enquiry flow.

Use source-specific links for attribution:

- `DURGA_PUJA_PUBLIC_PAGE_2026`
- `DURGA_PUJA_APP_BANNER_2026`
- `DURGA_PUJA_WHATSAPP_2026`
- `DURGA_PUJA_FB_POSTER_2026`
- `DURGA_PUJA_IG_POSTER_2026`
- `BSS_SOCIAL_2026`
- `SBSF_SOCIAL_2026`

Suggested WhatsApp message:

```text
Hi Cabbo, I want to book a Durga Puja pandal-hopping cab in Bengaluru.
```

### Booking Flow

No new campaign booking code should be written.

Customer action:

- Customer opens the public campaign page or WhatsApps Cabbo from the Whatsapp button OR Calls Cabbo from the number given in the campaign page.
- Cabbo checks availability manually.
- Cabbo confirms package, vehicle category, pickup time, and payment process.
- Confirmed booking is recorded in the campaign tracker.
- Driver is assigned manually and recorded in the spreadsheet tracker.

Final operating model:

> Online campaign page/post -> call or WhatsApp -> manual availability check ->
> confirmed booking in tracker -> trip completed -> customer pays Cabbo QR ->
> driver settlement -> reconciliation.

### Online Campaign Tasks

- [ ] Prepare the public campaign page with package details and manual enquiry
  flow.
- [ ] Prepare WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and customer-broadcast creatives.
- [ ] Use BSS social media support during the 15 September to 15 October booking
  window, like collaboration with BSS to post about the campaign multiple times.
- [ ] Add a temporary Cabbo landing/login page campaign banner during the booking
  window.
- [ ] Generate source-specific links for social, WhatsApp, app banner, and
  public page traffic.
- [ ] Close the campaign after 15 October and remove/disable active booking
  prompts.

## Track 2: Offline Flex Banner For App Recall And Adoption

### Objective

Use BSS and possibly SBSF pandal visibility to make attendees remember Cabbo as a
Bengaluru cab booking platform they can use later.

This track is for awareness and app adoption, not immediate Puja package
conversion. People seeing the standee during Puja may already be out visiting
pandals, so the physical banner should not spend space explaining Puja package
details.

### Sponsorship View

- BSS is the stronger current option because it offers a 4 ft standee/banner at
  a prime visibility area for `Rs 8,000`, social media presence, and support for
  the pre-Puja booking campaign.
- SBSF is optional and pending. Proceed only if they agree to `Rs 10,000` with a
  visible standee/banner and QR permission.
- Cabbo can participate in both BSS and SBSF with each location getting separate QR code
  source tracking.

### BSS Plan

- Use BSS as the primary offline visibility experiment.
- Design and print the flex/standee through Cabbo.
- Keep the standee app-first and reusable.
- Avoid year-specific text.
- Point the QR to the Cabbo app/homepage with BSS source tracking.

### SBSF Decision Rule

- Counter at `Rs 10,000` with one visible standee/banner placement included.
- Ask for placement at the main entry/exit gate, or near the
  pickup/drop-off/parking area.
- Request permission to include Cabbo logo, app link, WhatsApp number, and QR
  code.
- Skip SBSF if they cannot include a visible standee/banner at `Rs 10,000`.
- DO NOT SPEND `Rs 25,000` for SBSF.

### Flex Banner QR Target

The standee QR should open the Cabbo app/homepage, ideally with source tracking.

Examples:

- `https://app.cabbo.co.in/?utm_source=bss_standee&utm_campaign=durga_puja_2026`
- `https://app.cabbo.co.in/?utm_source=sbsf_standee&utm_campaign=durga_puja_2026`

Do not make the physical standee QR depend only on the Durga Puja campaign page,
because the standee should remain useful after the campaign.

## Track 1 Manual Booking Operations

The following pricing, payment, settlement, and tracker sections apply to the
online Puja booking campaign after an enquiry becomes a real booking. They do
not apply to the offline flex/standee app-recall track.

## Packages

| Package | Included distance | Small car | Large car |
| ------: | ----------------: | --------: | --------: |
| 4 hours | 40 km | Rs 2,000 | Rs 2,800 |
| 6 hours | 60 km | Rs 3,000 | Rs 4,200 |
| 8 hours | 80 km | Rs 4,000 | Rs 5,600 |
| 10 hours | 100 km | Rs 5,000 | Rs 7,000 |

Vehicle notes:

- Small cars: hatchback or sedan.
- Large cars: Ertiga, Innova, or similar.
- Vehicle model is subject to availability.

## Operating Windows

Day window:

- 9 AM to 7 PM
- Available packages: 4, 6, 8, and 10 hours

Evening window:

- 6 PM to 12 AM
- Available packages: 4 and 6 hours

Rules:

- Minimum booking: 4 hours.
- Maximum booking: 10 hours during the day.
- Maximum booking: 6 hours in the evening.

## Overage And Charges

Additional hour:

- Small car: Rs 500/hour charged to customer.
- Large car: Rs 700/hour charged to customer.

Driver additional-hour payout:

- Small car: Rs 400/hour.
- Large car: Rs 600/hour.

Additional kilometre:

- Customer and driver rates to be finalized for small and large cars.

Toll and parking:

- Charged at actuals.
- Passed through to the driver where applicable.

## Payment And Settlement

Money should enter Cabbo's account, not the driver's personal account.

Payment flow:

1. Driver records final trip duration, kilometres, toll, and parking.
2. Cabbo calculates the final customer payable amount.
3. Customer pays Cabbo's official QR code after the ride.
4. Customer payment reference is recorded against the campaign booking.
5. Cabbo remits calculated payout to the driver.
6. Driver payout reference is recorded for reconciliation.

## Campaign Tracker

Create the master campaign tracker before the campaign goes live.

The tracker should capture:

- Cabbo booking reference
- Customer name
- Customer phone number
- Source
- Service date
- Pickup time
- Pickup location
- Vehicle category
- Assigned vehicle
- Driver name
- Driver phone number
- Selected package
- Included hours and kilometres
- Actual hours and kilometres
- Base fare
- Overage charges
- Toll and parking
- Total customer payment
- Driver payout
- Cabbo gross revenue
- Customer payment reference
- Driver payout reference
- Status: enquiry, confirmed, completed, cancelled, or lost
- Remarks

Record types:

- Confirmed and completed trips are real manual/offline bookings.
- Enquiries that do not convert are soft leads and should be kept outside the
  master trip tracker, either in a separate soft-leads sheet or a lightweight
  follow-up list.
- Cancelled trips remain campaign records for learning and reconciliation.

After Puja, completed records can optionally be imported into Cabbo as
`manual/offline bookings` with campaign source `DURGA_PUJA_2026`.

## Existing Cabbo App

No permanent campaign booking flow should be built for this test.

App handling:

- Temporarily increase Bengaluru rental pricing for the Puja service dates if
  needed.
- Keep normal app rental prices broadly consistent with the advertised campaign
  rates.
- Customers who bypass the campaign poster may book through Cabbo's normal
  rental flow.
- Existing bookings must retain their original quoted price.
- Save the normal Bengaluru pricing configuration before any campaign change.
- Restore normal pricing immediately after the campaign period.

## Success Metrics

Online Puja booking campaign metrics:

- BSS social media enquiries from links posted from BSS in collab with cabbo.
- SBSF social media enquiries, if confirmed.
- Public campaign page visits from social media links posted from official cabbo handle.
- WhatsApp campaign enquiries from broadcasts.
- App banner clicks from the app landing or login.
- Total Puja campaign enquiries.
- Confirmed bookings.
- Completed trips.
- Cancelled trips.
- Lost leads and reasons.
- Average revenue per completed trip.
- Cabbo gross revenue.
- Driver payout accuracy.
- Customer feedback.

Offline flex/standee app recall metrics:

- BSS standee QR scans and homepage visits from BSS standee utm.
- SBSF standee QR scans and homepage visits from SBSF standee utm., if confirmed.
- WhatsApp or call enquiries that mention BSS/SBSF.
- New customer logins attributable to standee source links.
- Repeat/referral potential.

Minimum useful outcome:

- Learn whether Bengaluru Puja/pandal-hopping produces real cab enquiries.
- Complete a few manually controlled trips without operational confusion.
- Learn whether BSS/SBSF offline presence improves Cabbo app recall enough to
  repeat community sponsorships in various ocassions ahead.
- Build reusable local trust and content for Cabbo without confusing the two
  campaign tracks.

## Decision After Campaign

After Puja, answer:

1. How many Puja booking enquiries came from each online source?
2. How many online enquiries became confirmed bookings?
3. How many confirmed bookings were completed?
4. What package was most requested?
5. What vehicle category was most requested?
6. What questions did customers ask repeatedly?
7. How many app/homepage visits or enquiries came from BSS/SBSF standee links?
8. Did BSS/SBSF offline visibility produce enough app recall value to repeat
   community sponsorships?
9. Should seasonal booking pages remain manual, become semi-automated, or be
   skipped next time?
